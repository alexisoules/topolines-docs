# Getting started

Your first contour map takes about a minute. All you need is a free Google account.

## 1. Open the editor

Go to [topolines.app/app](https://www.topolines.app/app). You land on an interactive map.

## 2. Find your place

Use the search bar to jump to any location on Earth — a city, a mountain, a coastline, an address. You can also pan and zoom manually, and switch between **Map** and **Satellite** views at the bottom of the screen.

## 3. Draw a zone

Pick the **selection tool** in the bottom toolbar, then drag a box over the area you want.

The zone's surface area is displayed under it. If it turns **red**, the area exceeds the limit for your plan — just shrink it.

> **Tip:** smaller zones give denser, more legible relief. A 5–15 km box across a mountain range usually reads better than a whole region.

## 4. Generate

Hit **Generate Contours**. TopoLines fetches the elevation data for that exact bounding box and traces the contour lines.

## 5. Tune the look

Everything is adjustable live — see [Contour settings](contour-settings.md):

- **Contour Interval** — vertical distance between lines, in metres
- **Simplification** — smooths jagged, pixel-faithful lines
- **Stroke** — colour or elevation gradient, width, joins, taper
- **Annotations** — optional summits, rivers, place labels
- **Background** — colour or transparent

## 6. Export

Choose your format and hit **Export Selected**. See [Exports & credits](exports.md) for what's included in each tier.

- **PNG** — 1024 px free, up to 3200 px on paid tiers
- **SVG** — vector paths, paid tier, ready for Figma / Illustrator / laser cutting
